🏆 SIH — About This Project

Camp With Us is a Smart India Hackathon tourism platform built for the theme: "Student Innovation — A solution/idea that can boost the current situation of the tourism industries including hotels, travel and others."


The platform provides AI-powered itinerary generation, smart hotel discovery, multilingual assistance, emergency safety tools, local business promotion and a complete tourism information system — all connected end-to-end through a Node.js + MongoDB + Gemini AI backend.


Node.js + Express MongoDB Atlas Google Gemini AI JWT Auth HTML5 + CSS3 + JS Google Maps
